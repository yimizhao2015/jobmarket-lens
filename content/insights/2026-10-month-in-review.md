Based on data collected through October 26, 2026, JobMarket Lens counts 212,478 postings published from October 5 to October 25 by 440 S&P 500 companies on their own career sites. That is three full Monday-to-Sunday weeks, one more than in our [first look](/insights/2026-10-first-look), and 15 more companies.

## Three weeks of postings

| Week starting | Postings |
| --- | ---: |
| October 5 | 61,510 |
| October 12 | 87,565 |
| October 19 | 63,403 |

The week-to-week totals still reflect how the data was gathered as much as how companies hired. The first full collection ran on October 16, so postings from the first week that closed before then were never seen, and the latest week has been read only once so far. With a few more weeks of collections, these totals will start to show real trends.

## A quarter of October postings are already gone

48,529 postings (22.8%) published in October were no longer listed on the company's site by October 26. Among postings from the week of October 12, 33.8% had already closed. Closing rates were similar across occupations: 21.9% for management roles, 24.7% for sales, 25.1% for computer and mathematical jobs and 26.7% for transportation and material moving.

## The job mix held steady

Sales and related roles made up 19.4%, 21.1% and 19.0% of postings in the three weeks, and management roles about 15% every week. Across the month, sales leads at 20.0%, followed by management (15.4%), business and financial operations (11.1%), computer and mathematical occupations (9.1%) and architecture and engineering (8.3%). Healthcare roles grew from 2.3% of postings in the first week to 4.4% in the third.

The biggest posters are again large consumer-facing employers: Kroger (5,842 postings), CVS Health (5,817), AutoZone (5,728), Amazon (4,348) and Starbucks (4,101), followed by Lowe's, UPS, Accenture, Ulta Beauty and JPMorgan Chase.

## Pay: a third of postings say what the job pays

32.8% of October postings, and 44.2% of U.S. postings, state a pay range in U.S. dollars, the same share as in our first look. The median midpoint of those ranges, annualized, is $89,000.

## Degrees, remote work and credentials

107,993 postings state a minimum degree. 48.0% of them ask for a bachelor's degree and 44.4% for a high school diploma, and only 15.5% accept equivalent experience instead. Of the 92,828 postings that state a work arrangement, 63.3% are on-site, 27.7% hybrid and 9.0% fully remote, a split that barely moved from week to week. A security clearance remains the most requested credential (9,107 postings), ahead of a driver's license (5,888) and a commercial driver's license (4,767).

## Where the jobs are

71.0% of postings are in the United States, led by California (15,309), Texas (14,198), Florida (7,802) and New York (7,325). India is the next largest country with 12,406 postings, followed by Canada (5,054) and the United Kingdom (4,731).

> A posting counts as closed when its company's latest successful collection no longer lists it. Each collection reads at most the newest 3,000 postings per company, so for the largest employers some older postings may drop out of view while still open. Companies whose sites block automated access are left out, not estimated. Pay figures are posted ranges annualized for comparison (hourly × 2,080), not actual earnings.
