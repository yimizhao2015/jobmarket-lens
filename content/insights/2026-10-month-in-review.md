Based on data collected through November 2, 2026, JobMarket Lens counts 278,834 postings published from October 5 to October 31 by 441 S&P 500 companies on their own career sites. That is every week of October since tracking began, and 16 more companies than in our [first look](/insights/2026-10-first-look).

## Four weeks of postings

| Week starting | Postings |
| --- | ---: |
| October 5 | 61,535 |
| October 12 | 88,126 |
| October 19 | 64,096 |
| October 26 (through October 31) | 65,077 |

The week-to-week totals still reflect how the data was gathered as much as how companies hired. The first full collection ran on October 16, so postings from the first week that closed before then were never seen. With a few more weeks of collections, these totals will start to show real trends.

## A third of October postings are already gone

93,949 postings (33.7%) published in October were no longer listed on the company's site by November 2. Among postings from the week of October 12, 48.9% had closed, so about half of a week's openings come down within three weeks. Closing rates were similar for most occupations, around a third for management, sales, business and computer jobs, but higher for transportation and material moving (42.9%).

## The job mix held steady

Sales and related roles made up between 18.9% and 22.6% of postings each week, and management roles about 15% every week. Across the month, sales leads at 20.6%, followed by management (15.3%), business and financial operations (10.8%), computer and mathematical occupations (9.1%) and architecture and engineering (8.1%). Healthcare roles doubled their share, from 2.3% of postings in the first week to 4.6% in the last.

The biggest posters are again large consumer-facing employers: CVS Health (8,848 postings), Kroger (8,293), AutoZone (8,092), Amazon (5,923) and Lowe's (5,544), followed by Accenture, Starbucks, JPMorgan Chase, Walmart and O'Reilly Automotive.

## Pay: a third of postings say what the job pays

32.8% of October postings, and 43.8% of U.S. postings, state a pay range in U.S. dollars, the same share every week of the month. The median midpoint of those ranges, annualized, is $87,872.

## Degrees, remote work and credentials

142,368 postings state a minimum degree. 46.8% of them ask for a bachelor's degree and 45.7% for a high school diploma, and only 15.3% accept equivalent experience instead. Of the 120,988 postings that state a work arrangement, 63.5% are on-site, 27.5% hybrid and 9.1% fully remote, a split that barely moved from week to week. A security clearance remains the most requested credential (12,098 postings), ahead of a driver's license (8,025) and a commercial driver's license (6,249).

## Where the jobs are

71.7% of postings are in the United States, led by California (20,102), Texas (19,332), Florida (10,230) and New York (9,574). India is the next largest country with 16,775 postings, followed by Canada (6,118) and the United Kingdom (5,853).

> A posting counts as closed when its company's latest successful collection no longer lists it. Each collection reads at most the newest 3,000 postings per company, so for the largest employers some older postings may drop out of view while still open. Companies whose sites block automated access are left out, not estimated. Pay figures are posted ranges annualized for comparison (hourly × 2,080), not actual earnings.
