Based on data collected through October 17, 2026, the first full collection of JobMarket Lens covers 156,287 postings published since October 1 by 425 S&P 500 companies on their own career sites. Here is what stands out.

## Sales and management lead the openings

Nearly one in five postings (19%) is for a sales or related role, many of them store and customer-facing jobs at large retailers. Management follows at 16%, then business and financial operations (12%), computer and mathematical occupations (9.5%) and architecture and engineering (8.7%).

The biggest posters are consumer-facing employers. Lowe's, Amazon, Dollar Tree, CVS Health, Kroger, Ulta Beauty and Starbucks each list around 3,000 or more postings, which is also the per-company collection limit, so their true counts are higher.

## A third of postings disclose pay

33% of all postings, and 44% of U.S. postings, state a pay range in U.S. dollars. The median midpoint of those ranges, annualized, is $94,500. It varies widely by occupation:

| Occupation group | Postings with pay | Median midpoint |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Management | 9,337 | $156,000 |
| Computer and mathematical | 5,627 | $152,950 |
| Architecture and engineering | 4,685 | $127,000 |
| Business and financial operations | 5,492 | $98,000 |
| Healthcare practitioners and technical | 3,093 | $89,550 |
| Sales and related | 9,107 | $54,080 |
| Transportation and material moving | 1,548 | $47,840 |

> Posted ranges are what employers advertise, annualized for comparison (hourly × 2,080). They are not actual earnings.

## Degrees: a split between bachelor's and high school

Just over half of postings (82,672) state a minimum degree. Of those, 49% ask for a bachelor's degree and 43% for a high school diploma; master's degrees (2.6%) and doctorates (2.0%) are rare as minimums. Only 16% of postings that state a degree say equivalent experience is accepted instead.

## Majors: engineering, computer science and business

34,623 postings name a field of study. Engineering of any kind is named most often (11,584 postings), followed by computer science (9,466), business and management (9,023) and electrical and computer engineering (6,619). Many postings list several acceptable majors, and many more ask only for "a related field".

## Skills: communication first, data close behind

Communication is mentioned in 71% of postings and collaboration in 60%. Among specific skills, data analysis appears in 23% of postings, well beyond technology roles.

## Credentials that open doors

A security clearance is the most frequently named credential (7,135 postings), reflecting defense and aerospace employers. It is followed by a driver's license (4,187), a commercial driver's license (3,211), a registered nurse license (2,131), the PMP project management certification (1,784) and the CPA (1,320).

## Remote work is the exception

Of the 69,940 postings that state a work arrangement, 38% are remote or hybrid and 62% are on-site. Fully remote postings are just 9%.

## Where the jobs are

56% of postings are in the United States; the rest reflect these companies' global workforces. Within the U.S., California (9,266), Texas (8,159), New York (4,287) and Florida (4,285) have the most postings. Among postings that state experience, the median requirement is 4 years.

## About these numbers

Postings measure hiring intent, not hires, and coverage is not complete: some career sites block automated access and are shown as blocked rather than estimated. See the [Methodology](/methodology) for how each field is derived, or explore the numbers yourself on the [dashboard](/).
