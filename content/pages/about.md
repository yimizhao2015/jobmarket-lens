JobMarket Lens shows what America's largest employers are hiring for, from the job postings they publish on their own career sites.

Every week it collects the open postings of the S&P 500 companies and turns each one into comparable fields: occupation, job level, degree and major, experience, skills, licenses, pay, work arrangement and location. Explore them on the [dashboard](/), read the monthly [Insights](/insights), or download a free sample on [Get the Data](/data).

## What makes it different

- **Official sources only.** Every posting comes from the company's own career site, not a job board.
- **Open about gaps.** Sites that refuse automated access are shown as blocked. Nothing is estimated.
- **History over time.** Postings keep the date they were first seen and are marked closed when they disappear.

## Who makes it

JobMarket Lens is an independent project. It is not affiliated with S&P Dow Jones Indices or with any company whose postings it analyzes. Questions and corrections are welcome on the [Contact Us](/contact) page.
